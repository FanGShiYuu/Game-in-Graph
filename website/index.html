The idea

Make the right interactions visible before making a decision.

Mixed traffic is dynamic, heterogeneous, and locally coupled. Game in Graph represents vehicles and their conflicts as a Spatiotemporal Conflict Topological Graph (SCTG), then solves coordination at the scale where conflicts actually occur.

01

Conflict-aware

Edges carry spatial and temporal interaction information, with traffic rules reflected in their semantics.

02

Scalable by structure

Community detection separates dense conflict groups from weakly coupled traffic participants.

03

Cooperative by design

Subgroup decisions are organized around a potential-game formulation that connects individual and system utility.

Framework

Representation → simplification → decision.

The framework follows one repeated receding-horizon loop.

1

Representation

SCTG construction

Vehicles are nodes. Directed and undirected interaction edges encode precedence, following, and unresolved crossing conflicts. Edge weights describe conflict intensity over the planning horizon.

→
2

Simplification

Subgroup partition

Conflict-weighted community detection identifies high-conflict subgraphs. The partition is refreshed as vehicles enter, leave, or interactions become critical.

→
3

Decision

Multi-level equilibrium

Each subgroup solves a cooperative decision problem with safety, efficiency, and comfort terms. The joint action is propagated and the graph is rebuilt for the next step.

At every planning step

A compact loop for changing traffic.

State updates refresh the interaction region. The SCTG is rebuilt, communities are updated when the interaction pattern changes, and subgroup actions are evaluated before the next vehicle update.

  1. 01 Observe vehicle states and planned routes
  2. 02 Build and weight the SCTG
  3. 03 Update high-conflict communities
  4. 04 Solve cooperative subgroup decisions
  5. 05 Advance states and repeat
